AeroPredict Indian Aviation Analytics Tamil Nadu Network

End-to-end Machine Learning system predicting domestic flight delays across Coimbatore (CJB), Chennai (MAA), Madurai (IXM), Tiruchirappalli (TRZ) and major connecting metros. Developed in accordance with Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) 15-minute standard punctuality criteria.

Aviation Hubs

  • Coimbatore (CJB) - Peelamedu
  • Chennai (MAA) - Meenambakkam
  • Madurai (IXM) & Trichy (TRZ)
  • Kempegowda Bengaluru (BLR)

Evaluation & Models

  • Random Forest Classifier (Balanced)
  • XGBoost Classifier (Gradient Boosted)
  • Delay Duration Regressor (MAE ~10.5m)
  • REST API Enabled (/api/predict)
